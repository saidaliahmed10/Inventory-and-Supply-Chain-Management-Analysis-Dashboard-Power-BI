# 📦 Inventory and Supply Chain Management Analysis Dashboard

### 🌍 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyses inventory and supply chain operations covering inventory performance, warehouse utilization, transportation costs, lead times, sales trends, and order fulfillment across regions and product categories.

### 1️⃣ Business Problem
Organizations often face challenges in managing inventory and supply chain operations, including:

- Excess inventory holding costs
- Stock shortages and backorders
- High transportation expenses
- Inefficient warehouse utilization
- Long supplier lead times
- Difficulty tracking sales and inventory performance

Without proper visibility into these metrics, businesses may experience increased costs and reduced customer satisfaction.


### 1️⃣ Objectives
The objectives of this dashboard are to:
- Monitor warehouse utilization levels.
- Analyze inventory turnover performance.
- Track transportation costs across regions.
- Evaluate supplier lead times.
- Identify backorder trends.
- Compare inventory levels by category and region.
-Monitor yearly sales performance.
